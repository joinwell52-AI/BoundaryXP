# Contributing / 参与维护

Contributions concern the BXP specifications, research documentation and saved research examples. The workbench implementation remains private and is not shipped here.

Identify the document edition, exact claim, proposed correction and supporting evidence. Distinguish source interpretation, mathematical checking and presentation issues. Use synthetic reproductions; omit credentials, account details and private task data.

Preserve frozen protocols, source evidence, annotations, failures and reports. Do not regenerate the paper package or change material licenses during routine maintenance. Read the [maintenance rules](AGENTS.md), [documentation guide](docs/DEVELOPMENT.md) and [publication scope](docs/PUBLICATION-SCOPE.md).

中文贡献请注明版本、具体问题与证据，区分语义解释、数学核验和展示问题。工作台源码不公开，不把私有实现、现场配置、内部提示词或个人历史补入仓库；本次源码清理不改论文包、统计、标注或许可证。
