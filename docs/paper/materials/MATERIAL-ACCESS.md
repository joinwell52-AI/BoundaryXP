**Excluded implementation material:** 58618 source snapshots; Core, service, UI and launcher implementations; product aggregate reducers and their display modules; runtime dependencies; production prompt templates; live-workbench collection scripts. These remain separately managed engineering assets. Their historical identities may occur in saved experimental records, without supplying their contents.

The package manifest fixes every distributed file. Original project/result/report bytes are retained. The statistics entry reconstructs annotations and saved LLM judgments and audits retained calculator aggregate records. It does not execute the product reducer. Independent mathematical scripts recompute declared finite models and selected query operands; they do not replicate the complete product, regenerate natural-language analysis, or certify interpretation correctness.

Production-prompt digests and historical search results remain visible; the templates themselves are not distributed. The recorded draft.1 protocol text is included separately as protocol evidence, not as a runtime installation. The paper's method baseline remains draft.2.
