# Reusable mechanism proposals (before runtime changes)

## Iteration 1 — modal and organizational ownership

Hypothesis: candidate parsing currently loses a required action under a syntactic requirement governor, or binds the discourse subject “task” as an ordinary patient. Support/organization verbs then survive as independent actions. Fix ownership at candidate generation, before Γ.

Invariant: a directive/requirement governor licenses its bounded complement and explicit coordinated continuation. A task-disourse subject does not become its business object or participant. Organization is a responsibility STATE with source-supported AGENT; governed business predicates remain independent event candidates. Operators cannot survive as their own business events merely because NLP tagged them VV.

Why current mechanisms fail: governingDirective recognizes only polite requests; actorRange excludes task requirement prefixes, organizationalContext requires a preceding polite/modal token and uppercase dependency subject. Real HanLP variations split 由/PM/组织 differently. Dependency/SRL ownership and explicit source markers need to be combined, with provenance.

Primary gate: required complement has modal owner evidence; organization/requirement operator is absent from certified business-event use sites; explicit participant retained; Root50 unchanged. Mutations substitute nouns, files and human/role names, vary task/this task, Chinese/English and passive, and contrast requirement with quoted content and forbidden/withdrawn obligation.

Falsification: no reduction in the source-derived defect matrix, any false positive in quote/background or modal contrasts, or Root50 regression. Two non-improving iterations trigger method-preserving architecture review.
# Iteration 2: local modality and typed nominal ownership

Invariant: a source-local negative operator owns only its syntactic predicate;
`not required` withdraws an obligation, while `no X may be Y` prohibits Y.
An explicit negative responsibility is a role state and must not create a
positive performer role. An explicit nominal business complement retains its
own patient; organizational/support predicates do not replace that patient.

Existing failure: `no ... may be loaded` is treated as permission, plain task
negation loses prohibitions, nominal investigations/probes lose their local
file/environment, and a newly named negative approver incorrectly needs a
positive participation mention before being representable.

Mutation contract: two new artifacts and actors, Chinese/English equivalents,
positive/negative permission contrast, quoted negation control, and negative
role versus prohibited approval control. No case IDs or source offsets are
inputs to these mechanisms. Root-050 must remain 50/50.
# Iteration 3: governed nominal complements and bounded omissions

Invariant: an explicit directive/organization/support governor may license a
known nominalized business predicate even when the NLP labels it as a noun.
The local operand is the complement's file, prepositional object or modifier,
not the support verb, task noun or predicate's own name. Omitted read/review
arguments require a unique typed input/output domain; multiple operands keep
their explicit set connector and their provenance.

Failure hypothesis: noisy dependency attachment filters out the first nominal
predicate, qualified file operands cannot pass the pure-file recognizer, and
organizational execution bounds are obtained from already incorrect events.
Test obligations: changed actor/artifact names; transitive/nominal equivalents;
quoted noun mention negative control; two-output ambiguity contrast; mutation
of nominal qualifier without changing ownership; Root-050 preservation.
# Iteration 4: discourse annotations and closed source scope

Invariant: explicitly identified output content, content-only authorization
disclaimers and scalar-role explanations are preserved as typed states with
their owner and source; they do not become current execution. Explicit
exclusions in either language produce source-backed restrictions. This does
not license removing unresolved business arguments or genuine source doubts.

Failure hypothesis: output predicates with fronted patients and `其中` are
missed; explanatory sentences about a number are parsed as unsupported actions;
scope exclusions require an overly narrow surface form.
Mutations: fronted/postposed content, two independent content numbers, quoted
versus current commands, include/exclude polarity and multiple-output-owner
negative controls. The assertions must inspect typed content/scope, not merely
the absence of Gu.
# Iteration 5: structurally bounded missing arguments and temporal roles

Invariant: local read/review omission binds only a unique compatible prior
predicate argument domain inside the same document block. Transfer omission
uses a unique produced artifact. A temporal reviewer is kept as a participant
of a review state and the following consequent retains the exact temporal
guard. More than one candidate remains unresolved; no nearest-name choice.

Failure: permissible read constraints lack patients, explicit final-delivery
operators are not treated as directives, and cross-clause temporal review
states preserve conditions but lose reviewer use-sites.
Mutation controls: unrelated adjacent noun, two produced artifacts, new
participant names, optional read versus forbidden read, and temporal guard
removal. Assertions inspect role/argument IDs and shared condition provenance.
# Iteration 6: passive production and temporal proposition contracts

Invariant: source-declared deliverable identity and nominal creation are
production candidates, not guesses from filename suffixes. Passive production
retains its explicit patient as RESULT. Temporal review states retain typed
reviewers and both local antecedent/consequent layers, with a shared subject
only when the actual single-clause structure licenses it. Approval antecedents
remain states; they are not current approval actions.

Failure: nominal English creation and `The deliverable is X` have no RESULT;
reviews with omitted unique report operands fail the old two-subject check;
fronted condition clauses lose role endpoints under noisy noun tags.
Controls: new filenames, explicit versus mentioned output, quoted declaration,
two compatible report operands and multiple antecedent subjects. No entity or
case ID patterns may occur in runtime.

# Iteration 7: explicit passive operands and conditional budget ownership

Invariant: a passive typed subject is the local patient/result, not the
organization governor's task noun. A negative quantified passive subject owns
the prohibition and retains its complete input set. A conditional retry budget
has both an explicit execution operand and its original failure guard. Replaced
argument diagnostics cannot leave dangling unresolved references in plans.

Failure hypothesis: passive correction only supported production, stale native
patient scopes survived, and retry target replacement retained a diagnostic ID
that earlier quantity replacement had retired. Existing syntax markers were too
narrow to preserve nominal checks followed by a bounded retry consequent.

Controls: passive/active equivalent files and changed actors, both/either/neither
contrasts, quoted passive negative control; failed/succeeded guard polarity,
changed retry counts, upper/lower budget contrast, competing local domains.
All controls run before final runtime freeze, without Gold or Blind access.

# Iteration 8: state and content contracts at explicit clause boundaries

Invariant: completed-state antecedents stay separate from following production,
English postposed completion conditions retain both Boolean operands, and
acceptance counts are counts of the source state rather than execution budgets.
Document-only envelopes and section cardinality retain the actual artifact
owner. Permitted/required restricted execution has both its action and its
restriction; a scope exclusion never acquires a business action.

Controls: AND/OR, upper/lower count and source order mutations; only-content
versus current-action negatives; substituted section/file names; state
antecedent with and without a consequent. Root-050 remains independently gated.

Passive notification soundness correction: an explicitly notified person is
both the affected passive patient and recipient, not an artifact. Γ may certify
that patient only with a matching explicit passive-subject certificate and a
same-event recipient candidate. A generic PERSON patient remains rejected;
the frozen scope validator continues to demand an actual owning object edge.

# Iteration 9: preposed nominal arguments and per-relation quantity use-sites

Invariant: a governed nominal check/probe/query retains the noun before its
predicate and its own case-marked location. Adjacent read/check predicates are
a composite check with a read qualifier, not a read of the word “check”. A
LIMIT relation selects its own exact value expression; two task resources
cannot overwrite each other's quantity merely because they share a TASK head.

Controls: changed count/unit, swapped resource order, nominal versus transitive
check, and quoted compound controls. Multiple unrelated operands stay unknown.
## Iteration 10 — adjunct, valence and declaration contracts (predeclared)

Hypothesis: an explicit source-material preposition licenses an input endpoint independently of the direct patient; role declarations and delegation/concession operators are states, not business actions; an explicitly prohibited intransitive operation can denote its own operation domain without inventing an affected artifact. Temporal aggregation may inherit only the unique structurally owned output.

Invariants: preserve preposition and operand spans, predicate ownership and exclusion polarity; no job-title authority; no nearest-output rule; prohibition operation nominal is distinct from an artifact argument. Mutations change filenames, participants, prepositions, multiple output domains and positive/negative modality. Missing typed evidence stays unresolved. These mechanisms precede Gc freeze and are certified by unchanged downstream projection.
## Iteration 11 — local transition, bounded quantity and purpose roles

Hypothesis: an attestation followed by an explicit “之后方可” marker licenses a gate on the next same-sentence event; counts introduced with an execution budget have local execution targets, while minimum prerequisite counts remain acceptance conditions. Transfer-purpose review identifies a reviewer role without licensing external review execution. Relative “only after” applies to its syntactic governing review.

Invariants: no cross-block quantity transfer, no minimum accepted count converted into an upper budget, no recipient promoted to authority, no nearest actor, no unconditional rewrite of a guarded event. Mutation tests vary count values, upper/lower operator, execution types, actors and unrelated relative clauses. Earlier artifact ARG1/ARG2 adaptation and negative-role alias consistency are recorded as iteration-10 contract corrections; no previous evidence is replaced.
## Iteration 12 — procedural prerequisites and ambiguity evidence

Hypothesis: a minimum-count procedure followed by an explicit gate owns an execution prerequisite and its count; a repeated final-output reference aliases only a unique declared artifact. Explicitly governed quoted commands are executable propositions, whereas quoted report contents and source-ambiguity declarations remain data/states. Unresolved references enumerate typed input alternatives in the same structural domain; explicit approval ambiguity preserves ANY and ALL alternatives without choosing either.

Invariants: quote enclosure alone never authorizes execution; require/request governor evidence is necessary; no reference may borrow a recipient or a result from another block; ambiguity metadata is not a resolution. Mutations vary directive vs document-content governor, two/three input domains, subject names, count units and final-output cardinality. Clear outcomes remain resolved; only actual ambiguous operands remain unknown.

## Iteration 13 — independent whole-source review closes ownership assertions

The first automated ledger reached 100/100 assertions, but full source/Canonical review identified additional unsupported participants: metalinguistic task subjects and role phrases including organization governors. A source-material adjunct used a retired-node flag instead of live candidate ownership and could cross the next predicate into the produced artifact. An omitted review operand could bind to its own support noun instead of the unique explicit input in the bounded block. These are shared type/ownership defects, not new answer slots.

Invariant: task-reference subjects cannot become participants; role identity is separate from its predicate; adjunct operands stop at the next live predicate; an event token cannot be its own artifact object. Existing explicit input domains may bind an omitted complement only when exactly one compatible endpoint exists. New assertions and mutations use changed roles, file names, multiple input domains and source/produced-artifact contrasts. Earlier 100/100 is explicitly provisional assertion coverage, not final source semantic acceptance.
