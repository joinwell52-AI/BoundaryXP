# 界痕与7+1 / BoundaryXP and 7+1

界痕研究如何将任务要求、候选安排和已有事实表示为带原文证据的7+1关系，并对声明范围内的有限数学对象进行核验。I、O、A、P、R、V、C分别记录输入、交付、动作、责任分工、资源、版本依据、完成停止；B独立记录禁止边界。

BoundaryXP studies an evidence-linked 7+1 representation of requirements, proposed arrangements and reported facts, with finite mathematical checks under stated assumptions. I/O/A/P/R/V/C record inputs, outputs, actions, participants, resources, version/basis and completion/stopping; B independently records prohibitions.

未明确的信息保持未明确。引文匹配不等于语义蕴含，结构或计算有效不等于语义提取准确。业务裁决和执行授权由责任人与宿主系统掌握。

Missing information remains missing. Quotation matching does not establish semantic entailment; structural or mathematical validity does not establish extraction accuracy. Business decisions and execution authority remain with responsible people and host systems.
